## Platform

web

## Users

- Decision-makers who act on market research: investors, CEOs, and strategists, the site's three named audiences. Company leaders, strategy teams, sell-side analysts, and management consulting firms also buy the research.
- They arrive judging whether to trust an outside research lab with a question that matters: a market, a competitor set, a company, a trend.

## Product Purpose

White Sail Labs is an independent market research lab. It sells market research as a service and serves it on a subscription SaaS platform. Research it delivers keeps running on the platform instead of ending as a static report, and clients can serve themselves there: steer the research they were given and build their own.

Success on the website: a visitor understands that this is a research lab of people and agents, not an AI tool, and requests early access or gets in touch.

## Positioning

- Research as a service and a platform at once: framed and delivered by people, kept running by agents, extendable by the client.
- Built by two kinds of people. Applied AI Engineers are intelligence-agency veterans with expertise in applying AI to critical real-world problems, effectively and responsibly. Business Engineers are domain experts who frame the problem, define the solution the client needs, and steer the agents to make it happen. Together they build the Always-on Agents Fleet.
- The platform's world model keeps what it knows about every covered company current one fact at a time; each new fact is compared with what the model believed, and only the surprise propagates through the company's statements and along the value chain to its suppliers and customers. Signals are logged and their effect measured, and forecasts keep a track record against analysts' consensus. Approved for the site, with figures invented and labelled as illustrative.

## Operating Context

- Visitors are senior and short on time; they judge credibility within seconds, on a laptop or a phone between meetings.
- Actions on the site: Request early access (form), Get in touch (email options), Newsletter (form), Careers (three open roles: Forward Deployed Engineer in NY / US Remote, Applied AI Engineer and Software Engineer in Tel-Aviv). The site promises a reply within 1 business day.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Capabilities as the site states them: In depth (integrate signals once reserved for quant hedge funds), At scale (research every company and market you care about; no more shortlists), All the time (research agents work your questions 24/7), Your judgment (manage your own layers; apply your own judgment).
- Status: early access; the primary action is "Request early access".
- Stack: Next.js 16 (App Router), static export to GitHub Pages at white-sail.ai; the forms post to a Cloudflare Worker that writes to Notion, Slack, and Resend. Nothing renders on a server at request time.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: White Sail Labs.
- Logo: the sail mark (a sail silhouette with three speed lines) is fixed. Colours and typefaces are open for the website; the pitch deck's navy, cream, and amber and its Montserrat and Roboto Slab are not binding.
- Meaning of the name: a universal symbol of adventure, limitless exploration, and freedom.
- Line in use: "Your decisions. Better informed."

## Evidence on Hand

- Allowed: realistic invented demo content, labelled as illustrative; the world model and its mechanisms described above.
- People: one founder. A team portrait grid is not possible; the founder may appear by name, with a portrait to be supplied.
- Not available, never to be fabricated: client or design-partner logos, testimonials, customer counts, benchmarks, pricing.
- Screenshots of the real app are not approved for the site.

## Product Principles

1. People and agents together: never present the lab as a tool alone, or as people alone.
2. Research that keeps running: what we deliver stays live instead of ending as a report.
3. The client keeps the judgment: they can steer, extend, and apply their own view.
4. Demonstrate instead of claiming; label what is illustrative and invent no claims.
