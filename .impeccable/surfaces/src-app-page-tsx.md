# Home page

Scope: the White Sail Labs home page (`src/app/page.tsx`), redesigned into a new visual world. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience and job: investors, CEOs and strategists (also strategy teams, sell-side analysts and consultants) deciding whether this lab is worth a conversation. Primary action: request early access. Secondary: get in touch, newsletter, careers.

Proof and content: the world model's mechanism shown in full, with invented figures labelled Illustrative. The lab is presented as two disciplines plus the agent fleet; the single founder appears through a signed founder's note, not a lone headshot or a team grid. No logos, testimonials, customer counts, benchmarks, pricing or app screenshots.

Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/decision/steered-2.png` (comp round option one). Carried over from the other options at the user's request: the "Key · Who we are" strip from `.impeccable/mocks/home-comp-2.png`, placed below the first viewport; and the step-by-step visual from `.impeccable/mocks/home-comp-3.png` (numbered step rail, a large station turned over to show its forecast and record, fronts entering and leaving the frame) as a pinned scroll section, never the first viewport, on a second example: an oil-supply shock rippling through fuel into logistics and on to the companies that ship and fly.

Page order: hero (the ripple), Who we are (the chart's key strip), the step-by-step ripple (pinned scroll, oil shock), Capabilities (In depth, At scale, All the time, Your judgment), Built for, founder's note, closing early-access action. Capabilities must sit after Who we are.

Constraints: static export to GitHub Pages; the sail logo is fixed; no red anywhere (user steer); the surprise is carried in the brand gold. The illustrative scenario uses real company names with an invented event, so the Illustrative tag stays visible wherever it appears.

Memorable moment: a supplier's surprise rippling forward across the chart step by step, thinning as it travels.

Unresolved: founder's note copy and portrait; whether real company names stay in the illustrative scenario; display and text faces (measured from the approved comp); gold is for strokes and fills, never body text, because of its contrast on the light ground.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The economy as a synoptic pressure chart, where a supplier's surprise travels forward link by link through the world model, shrinking as it goes, and every effect is a forecast you can trace. Refuses the category's centred headline over a product-panel screenshot, and the dark network-graph look.

OWN-WORLD: A met-office chart's crisp off-white ground with fine navy isobars sweeping under everything; deep navy (#1a3263) and brand gold (#fab95b) are the only two inks, no red. Companies are station plots sharing one label grid (name, figure, change, time). Links are weather fronts: gold semicircles carry the surprise, navy triangles carry counter-effects, both thinning with distance. Reported facts are set solid, forecasts in hairline. A ruled title block holds the headline; a navy band holds the sail and navigation.

STORY: The visitor watches a supplier's signal (labelled illustrative) move through NVIDIA to its customers and a competitor. They understand that White Sail Labs is a research lab of people and agents keeping a living model, believe it shows what changes and why, and request early access.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A navy band across the top holds the cream sail, the wordmark, the navigation (Who we are, Capabilities, Careers, Contact) and a Request early access button at the right. The chart fills the rest. The numbered ripple runs across the upper two thirds: 1 SK hynix at left, 2 NVIDIA in the centre, 3a Microsoft and 3b Meta at right, 4 Samsung below right.
